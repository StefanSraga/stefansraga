SINCE 2015
This weekend, I headed to Rallye Prague Revival with my colleague Matěj, who’s also a photographer, and I was really excited to see what the event had in store for us. Amazing atmosphere, classic cars with real character, the smell of fuel, dust, puddles, mud… everything you’d expect and more. The whole event had such an incredible vibe and offered plenty of moments worth capturing. A perfect way to wrap up the weekend – full of adrenaline, photos, and inspiration.
